## Narrative

### positioning

Nexdata (NEXDATA TECHNOLOGY INC, California office [c102]; 'based in Singapore' per its About page [c016]) sells an off-the-shelf catalogue alongside collection and annotation services [c007]. It is technically linked to Datatang, the Beijing AI-data company with stock code 831428 [c087]: Nexdata pages embed a datatang.com hostname [c089]. No page states the corporate relationship. Nexdata says it was founded in 2011 and Datatang says 2010 [c007][c087].

### supply

Supply is mainly Nexdata's own production: a Physical AI data factory it says is 10,000+ m² [c003], collectors wearing Pico headsets [c094], phone owners recruited for projects [c097], 20,000+ annotators [c010] and Datatang's crowd platform Shujiajia [c105]. Large photo and video sets are licensed in from creators, photographers and TV sources 'through authorized channels' [c018][c083][c084][c085], but the channel is never named. A Linkerbot alliance co-creates dexterous-hand data [c099]. The partner programme recruits sellers, not data suppliers [c071].

### object_model

The unit is a dataset listing with a specification table (size, demographics, device, format, accuracy) [c030][c032]. Buyers can buy part of a dataset [c091]. The research template lists datasets by ID with a market value in USD [c051]. No versioning or revision model is published.

### listing

Listings are public, with specs, a few sample images and the line 'This is a paid dataset licensed for commercial use' [c025]. The buttons are 'Get Data Samples' and 'Ask for a Quote' [c027][c026]. Face listings give nationality and demographic counts but say nothing about consent [c031][c033]. Headline sizes: 30 million videos [c019], 1,000+ to 1,500+ datasets [c009][c095].

### discovery

Category pages filter by task type and modality [c036] and offer a downloadable catalogue request and 'Contact Sales' [c037]. A second storefront on Datarade (data.nexdata.ai) lists 159 products [c077].

### trust

Trust rests on Nexdata's assertions: GDPR/CCPA compliance [c015], per-listing GDPR/CCPA/PIPL statements [c035], 'consent with proper authorization' [c075], ISO 27001/27701 [c068], and iBeta certification for anti-spoofing data [c086]. No consent documents are shown. The website terms give everything 'as is' [c042], and the research template gives no warranty from Nexdata [c059].

### transaction

Every purchase is sales-led. A form asks for a company email and a data-requirement description [c028][c029]. Purchases are subject to Nexdata's acceptance [c039], and licensing is discussed before purchase [c012]. Datarade pages also end in 'Request Information' [c081]. Research datasets are released after a form and review [c063].

### pricing

nexdata.ai publishes no prices. On its Datarade storefront, products carry one-off 'Starts at' prices: USD 20K for most, including the video datasets [c078]; USD 10K for face and anti-spoofing sets [c080]; USD 100K for 100,000 hours of ego-centric data [c079]. Monthly, yearly and usage-based licences are marked not available.

### licence

Two tracks. Research: a published template that is free, non-exclusive, non-transferable and non-commercial, with no derivatives (models included) [c048][c049], one year [c054], confidentiality [c058], attribution with notice [c056] and a speech-set cap of 4 per applicant [c062]. Converting to commercial use means paying full market value [c052]. Commercial: 'tailored' by application, geography and usage scenario [c011]; its text is not public. The website terms bar resellers [c040].

### custody

The data is copied to the buyer. The research licensee holds copies and must destroy them [c055]; the catalogue promises delivery 'in seconds' [c103]; Datarade lists S3, SFTP and API delivery [c104]. Nexdata protects its own hosting with URL encryption, anti-hotlinking and anti-crawling [c069].

### vetting

Quality control is internal: self-inspection, then initial inspection sampling at least 50%, then final inspection sampling 30%, under ISO 9001 [c065][c066][c067]. Listings state label accuracy, for example above 97% [c032] and above 95% [c034]. No independent audit is cited apart from iBeta [c086].

### contributor_pay

Not published. Case studies describe recruiting UK iPhone owners [c097] and headset-wearing collectors [c094], and Datatang runs a crowd-task platform [c105], but no page says how any of them, or the creators of licensed-in works [c021], are paid.

### post_sale

Research licensees must destroy all copies within 5 days of expiry and certify it [c055]. Leakage triggers full market value plus per-quantity damages [c053]. The website terms incorporate return and shipping policies that are not linked [c043]. Nothing covers data-subject erasure after sale.

### catalogue_custom

The navigation splits 'Off-the-Shelf Datasets' from 'Data Service' [c017], and press uses 'Bespoke data collection services' [c006]. Nexdata frames the catalogue as the fast start and custom collection as the gap-filler [c092][c091]. Custom work comes with an NDA and 'data destroyed upon delivery' [c074][c076]. Stated capacity: 50,000+ hours/month of bespoke UMI data [c004].

### changes

Active: news dated 17 Sep 2026 announces an AI-data-infrastructure award [c002][c001]. The facility claim grew from 8,000+ m² (Jul 2026) [c096] to 10,000+ m² (Sep 2026) [c003]. The strategic direction is Physical AI, including a Linkerbot alliance [c099]. The website terms date from May 2022 [c038].

### demand

Nexdata claims 1,000+ customer companies [c008]. Its one described buyer pattern is to buy part of the catalogue to start, then commission custom collection [c091]. Universities receive free speech data and are listed as logos [c061][c057]. There is a partner programme to extend sales abroad [c071]. None of this is independently verified.

### regulation

Nexdata's privacy policy covers site visitors and customers only [c100], and it shares customer data within its corporate group [c101]. The research template sends disputes to a Chinese 'people's court' [c060]; the website terms impose arbitration [c041].

## Buyer journey

1. Lands on nexdata.ai: 'Off-the-Shelf Datasets' and 'Data Service' menus side by side, with a claim of 1,000+ ready datasets 'delivered in seconds'. [c017, c009]
2. Browses a category page (for example Computer Vision), filters by task type and modality; no prices are shown; 'Get Datasets Catalog' and 'Contact Sales' buttons. [c036, c037]
3. Opens a listing: spec table, stated label accuracy, a few embedded sample images and 'This is a paid dataset licensed for commercial use'. For licensed-in video, rights are described only as copyright-cleared works 'released by the author'. [c030, c032, c025, c020, c021]
4. Clicks 'Get Data Samples' or 'Ask for a Quote' and fills a form: name, phone, company, company email, and a requirement of at least 5 words. [c027, c026, c028, c029]
5. Alternatively finds the same products on Nexdata's Datarade storefront with 'Starts at' one-off prices (USD 10K to 100K), but still presses 'Request Information'. [c078, c079, c081]
6. Negotiates with sales: licence tailored to application, geography and usage scenario. The purchase is subject to Nexdata's acceptance; resellers are refused. [c011, c012, c039, c040]
7. Receives a copy of the data (the delivery mechanism for commercial sales is not documented) under an unpublished commercial licence with Nexdata Technology Inc. [c103, c046]
8. Academic route instead: applies through the sponsored-datasets form (at most 4 speech sets), signs the free one-year research licence, and must credit Nexdata, keep the data confidential and destroy it at expiry. [c061, c062, c063, c054, c056, c055]
